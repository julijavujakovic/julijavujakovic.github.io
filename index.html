About Me

A few things
worth knowing.

I have an MBA in Data Analytics and an undergraduate degree in psychology. The psychology degree shapes how I work more than people expect. Knowing why people make the decisions they do changes how I present data, and it changes what questions I ask before I start. I also spent 5 years as a Division I basketball team captain and 8 years on Luxembourg's national women's basketball team. I show up, I lead, and I don't drop things when it gets difficult.

I grew up in Luxembourg speaking 5 languages. Switching between cultures has been normal since childhood, and it carries into the work. You get better at figuring out what someone actually needs from a report versus what they asked for.

I'm drawn to healthcare, mental health, and tech, though I'm genuinely open to other industries too. Data problems worth solving exist everywhere, and the energy sector is one I'm actively exploring.
